### Hi, I'm Sergio

I'm curious about how people decide what to trust, and how a well made thing, a brand, a product, or a page, can quietly change how a business feels from the outside.

I'm happiest when I'm turning a question into something real that people can use. For more than two years I ran Sergio & Co., a go-to-market and design studio, working with clients across the US and Latin America.

I'm based in San Francisco, building at the intersection of design and technology. Lately I'm especially interested in what AI makes possible for small teams that want to build carefully.

When I'm not building, I'm usually writing. I publish longer essays on [Substack](https://sergwrites.substack.com) and shorter pieces as [@serg.writes](https://www.instagram.com/serg.writes) on Instagram, where I think out loud about AI, business, and creativity.

Come say hi at sayhi@sergiopineros.com. I'm always up for a good conversation or an ambitious idea.
